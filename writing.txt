## Finished: Your Family Is Already Disapointed in You
Type: Screenplay
Summary: A dark comedy about a lonely bassist kidnapped by a very determined band, written with Abi and Lebron in a screenwriting class. The prompt was to find a Craigslist ad and turn it into a story.
Link: your-family-is-already-disapointed-in-you.pdf
Inspiration: https://www.craigslist.org/about/best/ash/7608963933.html
---
EXT. WALMART PARKING LOT. MORNING.

JUSTIN walks out of a Walmart with shopping bags looped on both of his arms. They contain a sad amount of cup noodles and bottles of gatorade.

A beat-up, shady-looking white van pulls up right nearby. OTTO exits from the driver's side, cigarette in hand while sauntering towards Justin.

OTTO
What's up, lil' bro?

JUSTIN
Uh. Shopping. Nice to meet you.

OTTO
(dismissive)
Cool, cool, that's cool. Well, we were just about to set off for a gig. We're a band, you see. Rizzky Biznizz.

JUSTIN
Cool?

OTTO
You should join us.

JUSTIN
(smiling hesitantly)
Um. What?

OTTO
(suddenly threatening)
Get in the van.

JUSTIN
Sorry, what!?

The back doors of the van bust open to reveal KRIS, LANCE, and DUCKY. The three band members roughly grab Justin and haul him into the van while he kicks and screams. They muffle him with a towel, which knocks him out.

OTTO
Good work, team.

LANCE
How'd you know he'd be shopping here?

OTTO
He always shops here. Every week on Tuesday at 2 PM and he always buys blue gatorade and chicken noodles. $18.99.

LANCE
(looking around)
Where's his car?

INT. JUSTIN'S CAR

Kris shatters Justin's car windows with a spiked bat. The alarm is blaring, but because this is a Walmart, no one cares. Ducky rummages through the trunk until he finds a large bass, still in its case. They nod to each other and walk back.

INT. OTTO'S VAN

Justin groggily comes to his senses, vision hazing around the edges. Lance is driving, while Otto, Kris, and Ducky stand over him with intense stares.

JUSTIN
(out of it)
What...? Hello...?

OTTO
We gotta play in two hours. You gotta get ready?

JUSTIN
Who...?

OTTO
You, bitch.

Justin glances around the van. It is clearly well-lived in, leather seats tearing apart and assorted junk food wrappers everywhere. He takes note of the bandmates' crazy expressions, and visibly shrinks back.

JUSTIN
(laughing nervously)
What's in it for me?

OTTO
Uh, fame? Fucking dumbass.

JUSTIN
...Why me?

OTTO
You need this. You live a boring fucking life, you ran away from home like, 3 weeks ago, you live in a single room apartment, 1B, you're late on rent, your fridge is leaking, your window doesn't lock which makes it conveniently easy to break into--

KRIS
Go easy on him. The guy'll learn.

OTTO
Ugh. Fact of the matter is, you play good, and we need a bassist. Got it?

JUSTIN
(strangely resigned)
You guys are insane.

INT. FANCY BAR BACKSTAGE. NIGHT.

The bar is bustling. Voices and clinking glasses can be heard behind the large curtain separating the band from the public. Fancy LEDs line the ceiling, fairy lights hung everywhere else.

Justin is frantically practicing with sheet music he was given only 2 hours ago when the backstage lights turn off.

JUSTIN
(startled)
What the fuck?

OTTO (O.S.)
Everyone get together, right the fuck now.

Lance walks in holding many lit candles while Otto hands out various assorted animal masks. They are creepy at first glance, but are made of paper mache and held together by duct tape.

JUSTIN
What--

OTTO
(cutting off, non-explanatory)
Good luck ritual.

Lance sets the candles on the floor in a circle, the bandmates standing around them.

Justin hesitantly puts his goat mask on as everyone holds hands.

ALL BUT JUSTIN
(incomprehensible chanting)

JUSTIN
(about to shit his pants)
