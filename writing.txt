HOW TO USE THIS FILE
Each piece starts with a line beginning "## ".
Write "## Finished:" for finished writing, or "## Idea:" / "## Drafting:" for the Ideas on my mind cards.
Under that, fill in the short details (leave any blank), then a line with three dashes (---).
Paste your full writing below the dashes. Line breaks stay exactly as you type them.
To add a new piece, copy a whole block and change it. To remove one, delete its whole block.
Nothing above the first "## " line shows on the site.
